Tower Building (/tower) is ported from the open-source game "tower_game" by iamkun / BMQB, Inc:
https://github.com/iamkun/tower_game

The game rules and all images, sounds and the "wenxue" font in this folder come from that repository's
`assets` directory. The engine was rewritten in TypeScript after the original `src/` and its small canvas
engine cooljs (https://github.com/iamkun/cooljs, ISC license, by iamkun).
