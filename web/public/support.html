FAQ常见问题

How do I sign up?怎么注册？

Open the app and fill in a nickname, age and gender. Your account is linked to your device, so no password is needed. You must be 18 or older.

打开 App，填写昵称、年龄和性别即可。账号与设备绑定，不需要密码。仅限年满 18 周岁的用户使用。

How do I log in again?怎么重新登录？

Open the app on the same device and you are signed in automatically. If you changed devices, contact us.

在同一台设备上打开 App 会自动登录。如果换了设备，请联系我们。

How do I report content or a user?怎么举报内容或用户？

Long-press the message or post and choose "Report", or contact us with the details.

长按消息或动态，选择「举报」；也可以把情况发给我们。

How do I delete my account?怎么注销账号？

Contact us and we will delete your account and its data.

联系我们，我们会注销你的账号并删除相关数据。
