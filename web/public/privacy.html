Privacy Policy隐私政策

HeartChat (the "Platform") takes your privacy seriously. This Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information. 1. Information we collect (1) Registration information: nickname, avatar, age and gender; (2) Device information: device identifier (used to bind and recover your one-account-per-device account); (3) Location information: with your permission, used for local content recommendations and showing distance; (4) Real-name information: name and ID number voluntarily submitted by female users (used only for ID verification); (5) Usage records: chat messages, posts, call records and points history. 2. How we use information (1) To provide core features: messaging, voice and video calls, posting and order matching; (2) Local recommendations and distance calculation; (3) Account security and handling of violations; (4) Points settlement and reconciliation. 3. Storage and protection (1) Chat messages are encrypted in storage and in transit; (2) Real-name information and ID numbers are stored encrypted, and staff cannot view the full information; (3) Data is stored on servers located in China. 4. Information sharing We will not sell your personal information to any third party. We share it only when: (1) we have your explicit consent; (2) it is required by laws and regulations or lawfully requested by regulatory authorities. 5. Your rights (1) You can change your nickname, avatar and other profile details at any time; (2) You can delete your own posts and clear your chat history; (3) To delete your account, please contact customer support. 6. Protection of minors The Platform does not provide services to users under 18. If we find that a minor is using the Platform, we will immediately delete the account and its information. 7. Policy updates This Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on the Platform.
